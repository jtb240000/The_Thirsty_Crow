You are a crow flying over a desert. You have been flying since early in the morning and have gotten very thirsty, and now the sun is beating down on you. Because you are a bird, you aren't able to carry any supplies with you. You have a long way to go and you might not make it if you don't get some water. You are far enough into the desert that your chances aren't any better if you turn back now.

What will you do?

  • Fly straight ahead in what you think is the shortest way out
  • Continue flying towards your destination
  • Fly down and take a break
  • Fly around and look for anything that might help
  • Fly back the way you came
